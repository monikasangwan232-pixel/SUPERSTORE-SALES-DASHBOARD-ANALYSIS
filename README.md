# Superstore Sales Dashboard Analysis
Where does the business make money, and where does it lose it?
This project analyses 2011-2014 Superstore sales data with an interactive Google Data Studio dashboard and turns the findings into profit-improvement recommendations.
## Dataset
- Period: Jan 2011 - Dec 2014
- 9,994 order lines, 5,009 unique orders, 21 columns
- 4 regions (East, West, Central, South) and 3 categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies)
## Approach
1. **Clean:** prepared the dataset in Google Sheets
2. **Measure:** defined KPIs (sales, profit, margin, orders)
3. **Visualise:** built an interactive dashboard in Looker Studio
4. **Recommend:** linked each recommendation to a dashboard finding
## Key findings
- Total sales of $2.30M and profit of $287K, a 12.5% margin
- West is the largest region; South is the smallest, at about half of West
- Sales are evenly split across categories, but profit is not: Technology gives 51% of profit and Office Supplies 43%, while Furniture gives only 6%
- Sales are seasonal, with Q4 peaks every year and a clear upward trend
## Recommendations
1. Fix Furniture profitability (review pricing, costs and discounting)
2. Focus on Technology and Office Supplies
3. Grow the South region
4. Plan stock and campaigns around the Q4 peak
